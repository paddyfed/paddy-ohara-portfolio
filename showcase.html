Project Showcase

This is where I share some of the bigger projects I’ve taken on and the stories behind them. Each one pushed me to try something new, whether it was capturing a brand on camera, building a clean and simple website, or designing visuals that tied everything together. These projects represent the kind of work I love doing and the creativity, problem-solving, and collaboration that go into making it happen.

Project 1

Description

Monaghan Institute's Learning Hub

About

Monaghan Institute created a designated study area in the college and they wanted to showcase this. They asked me to help create a promotional video for this Learning Hub using learner and staff interviews. The video was shown at the Cavan and Monaghan ETB FET Showcase in December 2025.

Process

The script was created by Monaghan Institute staff and we organised a day to record all the lerners and staff interviews in the learner hub room. Once all the interviews were recorded, I used Premiere Pro and After Effects to edit and create the video. We used drone shots of the college and recorded all the b-roll footage ourselves.

Programs Used

  • Adobe Premiere Pro
  • Adobe After Effects

Project 2

Description

Poc The Blues Music Festival promotional items

About

Poc the Blues is a music festival that takes place every year in The Poc Fada bar in Monaghan town. They asked me to create some promotional material for the festival in 2025. This included a new logo for the festival, posters for print, promotional videos for the acts, mockups for merchandise, and other social media items.

Process

Most of the work was done in Adobe Illustrator. This is where the posters, logos, and social media items were created. Premiere Pro was used to create the promotional videos. Canva was used to generate the merchandise pictures. Feedback was important as there was a lot of back and forth at the beginning of the project as I tried to get the right tone for the posters.

Programs Used

  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Adobe Premiere Pro
  • Canva

Project 3

Description

Curious Cubs app promotional video

About

Curious Cubs is an Android and iOS app for young children to learn about songs, dancing, colouring, and music. I was asked to create some promotional videos for the app to be used in social media and in the app store.

Process

As the app was still in development, I was able to get a test version on an iPad where I was able to screen record different areas of the app. I used these clips to stitch together the promotional video. The voiceover was supplied to me and the final version would have background music that was being created by someone else. I created both a landscape and portrait version of the video depending on whether it was for mobile or not.

Programs Used

  • Adobe Premiere Pro
